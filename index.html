Methods & Limitations

Measurement Methods

Heart rate and HRV are measured in BPM using a physiological sensor. Ambient temperature and relative humidity are measured using environmental sensors.

Combined Fatigue Score (CFS)

The CFS is a prototype index (0-100) combining physiological and environmental indicators to represent the observed workload. Calculation method pending industrial engineering validation.

Validation Plan

Compare readings against reference instruments, verify data transmission, and evaluate dashboard.
